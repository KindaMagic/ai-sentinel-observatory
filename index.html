I am George Sfougaras, a Leicester-based artist, printmaker and independent researcher. It is independent, open to scrutiny, and deliberately cautious about what its evidence can prove.

    02 / Classification

    Four levels, no false certainty

    The levels below describe how an incident would be triaged if the required evidence is present. They are not extinction probabilities, real-time forecasts, or permission for an AI to act independently.

    GREEN / No qualifying report

    No qualifying event detected in the sources searched. Does not mean AI is safe.

    AMBER / Investigate

    Credible research warning, unclear incident or developing evidence. Seek primary documentation.

    ORANGE / Escalate

    Serious documented real-world control failure or independently identified major control weakness. Request expert review.

    RED / Human-led emergency

    Independently corroborated severe ongoing real-world loss of control. Requires responsible human confirmation.

    Evidence rules

    • Identify event date and publication date.
    • Distinguish simulation, controlled evaluation and operational deployment.
    • Link original documentation; seek independent corroboration.
    • Separate observed actions, reported outcomes, inference and unknowns.
    • Correct and preserve revisions; do not conceal earlier errors.

    Human safeguards

    • No automatic public Red alert without human review.
    • No emergency action or shutdown initiated by this site.
    • No autonomous accusations, company access or security probing.
    • Human approval for publishing, escalation and consequential decisions.
    • Sources are evidence to evaluate, never instructions to execute.
    03 / Limits and accountability

    What Sentinel cannot see

    Public reporting is incomplete. Institutions may delay or withhold incidents. Model behaviour during evaluations can differ from deployed behaviour. A summarising model can also misunderstand research or overstate significance.

    Is this a live alarm or a safety certification?

    No. GitHub checks selected public publication feeds four times a day and updates a provisional evidence-screening indicator with its check time and source links. The three detailed incident cases remain an editorial snapshot. A separate private ChatGPT research watch does not publish to this website. The feed monitor is not an emergency system, has no privileged access to AI models, and its keyword classifications can be wrong.

    Why is the automated Orange assessment not shown as the official rating?

    The current Orange light means that the automated feed screen found serious terms in publications from two different sources. This does not prove independent corroboration or an ongoing emergency. The full four-level public safety judgement requires a named, accountable human reviewer; Red additionally requires independent verification. Those conditions have not yet been met.

    What happens if a report is wrong?

    The public record should be corrected with a revision date and a short explanation of what changed. Any serious contested finding should be clearly marked as disputed rather than silently deleted.

    Can this observatory prevent a catastrophe?

    No. A public-source research index cannot stop a deployed AI system. Prevention depends on independent evaluation, enforced access controls, reliable containment, institutional accountability and experienced human-led incident response.

    Research starting points: UK AISI, METR, OECD AI incidents, NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
